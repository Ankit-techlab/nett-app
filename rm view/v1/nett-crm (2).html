GLOSSARY

Every dot, pill and line — decoded.

MOMENT & SIGNAL DOTS

Green — fresh consent, just approved
Gold — client wants to talk, waiting on you
Orange — follow-up due
Red — overdue or a drop-off; clear these first
Grey — preparation, no client waiting

CONSENT PILLS

CONSENTEDPortfolio, insights and briefing open — revocable anytime
LINK SENTWaiting on the client — only they can approve
NO CONSENTContact, bookings and comms only — numbers stay theirs

LEAD STAGE VS COMMUNICATION STATUS

Two separate axes. Stage = where the deal is (New → Contacted → Qualified → In progress → Won / Lost / Dormant). Communication status = whether the conversation is healthy (Replied · Awaiting reply · Follow-up due · Overdue). A lead can be Qualified and still Overdue — the red pill always wins your attention.

SCORES, BANDS & THE LINE

Clients carry a wealth health score (0–100); you carry a work health score built the same way. The depth gauge is "the line" — the gold marker is where they (or you) stand; the milestone label is the next line to cross. Bands: Good · Fair · Watch · Critical. NO DATA is never shown as a passing state — and an error is not NO DATA: errors show last-good data with a timestamp and a Retry; NO DATA asks for consent.

THE WATER

The vessel fills as your day’s moments complete. Views rise from below the waterline when they open. "Below the waterline" on a client page means the client hasn’t shared — it opens the moment they do. The Arth mark — a circle half-above the waterline — marks every surface where Arth is speaking; lavender belongs to Arth alone.

TRACE IDS
