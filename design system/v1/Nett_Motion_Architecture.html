NETT · MOTION ARCHITECTURE · FOR THE TEAM

Water moves.
Interfaces don't bounce.

Every animation in Nett is water doing something water does: rising, drifting, filling, settling. If a proposed animation can't be described that way, it doesn't belong.

1 · Philosophy — three laws

Law 1 — Motion is meaning. Something moves only when its state changed: a view surfaced, a day progressed, Arth started thinking, water rose. Decoration-motion is banned; if nothing changed, nothing moves.

Law 2 — Motion is honest. Loaders exist because a wait is real (the 5-minute AA read, Arth composing). We name the wait, show its progress truthfully, and release the user when we can. Motion never fakes speed and never manufactures delay for drama.

Law 3 — Motion is calm. Water has no bounce, no spring, no overshoot. Easings are ease / ease-out / ease-in-out. Anything elastic reads as a game; this is someone's money.

3 · When to use what — the decision table

SituationMotion
New view / sheet openssurface — always, automatically. Never a slide-from-side, never a zoom.
Arth is thinkingWater loader. Never a spinner, never "..." dots.
Work completedVessel level rises. A toast confirms in words + one ✓. No confetti, no fireworks — completing money tasks is dignity, not a slot machine.
Identity moment (open, sign-in)The one place motion gets a full second. Once per session, then out of the way.
Error appearsNo animation. Errors arrive still — motion on a failure reads as alarm. The toast slides in on the standard transition only.
Numbers change (scores, money)No count-up tickers. The number is the truth; it appears as the truth.

4 · Rules that never bend

Do — honor prefers-reduced-motion: every animation off, no exceptions. This is shipped and audit-checked on both surfaces.
Do — keep loops long (7s+) and amplitudes tiny for anything ambient. If a user can describe the animation, it's too loud.
Do — pair every loader with words that say what's actually happening ("Reading 24 months of bank flows…"), never a bare spinner.
Don't — bounce, spring, overshoot, parallax, shake, pulse-for-attention, or animate color. Water does none of these.
Don't — animate serious states: errors, compliance flags, mis-sell warnings, NO DATA. Stillness is the signal that this matters.
Don't — add a new keyframe without sign-off. The inventory above is the complete, closed set; extending it is a Design System change with a release note, not a CSS commit.
