v1.8 06 Aug 2026 · 11:55 IST

All 65 screens validated against nett-ux-writing v1.0. 46 patch groups, 368 replacements, copy only — structural parity with v1.7 verified element by element.

THE HEADLINE

The app's writing was already the strongest of the three surfaces — no urgency words, no guarantees, no drowning language, no "you should", and Nett was never used as a verb. Two real canon breaches were found, and one pre-existing problem that is bigger than either.

Canon breaches found

A · Peer comparison was shipped — 9 instances. "Profiles like yours average 58", "profiles like yours surface ≈₹6.9L/yr", and the same construction inside the consent gate, the scan entry and the confidence calculator. Three carried rupee amounts. Peer comparison is unauthorised pending founder + Yogesh sign-off, and §3 names this exact shape as inducement dressed as insight. Converted to benchmark framing: "The average is 58", "≈₹6,90,000 a year typically hides here".
B · Arth spoke in the first person. The chat empty state read "Ask me anything about your money… I answer from your data, set up the execution, and bring Swapnil in", and the daily thought offered "ask me why". §6: Arth states, shows and calculates — it has no self to refer to. Now: "Answers come from your data" and "see the working", which is also a canon button label. Swapnil's first person was left untouched — Human register is the one place "I" belongs.

The numerals migration — 232 figures

112 unique lakh figures converted to full Indian-grouped figures, from ₹0.4 L → ₹40,000 up to ₹96 L → ₹96,00,000. Every one verified below ₹1 crore, so nothing should have stayed in Cr. Alongside: 74 rates and flows given their basis ("42% a year", "₹2,890 a month"), 10 abbreviated durations spelled out, and three dollar figures unabbreviated.

With web v0.16 and app v1.8 done, the CRM is now the only unmigrated surface. Until it moves, the app and the CRM print the same numbers differently — which §5 forbids.

Jargon — 25 terms translated

XIRR → "return a year" (×12) · CAGR → "a year over 3 years" · drawdown → "fall" · liquidity → "cash you can reach" · bps → percentages · runway → "shortens the home loan" · total expense ratio → "yearly fee on this fund".

Buttons

Two "Next" buttons named what they actually do: "See how it works" on the intro carousel and "Send me the code" on the scan entry — which also sets the correct expectation before an OTP arrives.
